// About

From Schematic to Firmware to Vision

I'm a Mechatronics Engineer from Kairouan, Tunisia, graduated from ENICarthage in 2025. I work across the full stack of a device: designing circuits and PCBs in Altium Designer, programming STM32 and ESP32 firmware in Embedded C, and building computer-vision systems with Python, OpenCV and YOLO. My projects range from a 6-DOF Stewart platform and a 36 V combat robot to an IoT smart pet feeder and a license-plate recognition pipeline. I'm now deepening my firmware skills and looking for an embedded systems, hardware design, or AI & computer vision engineering role.
